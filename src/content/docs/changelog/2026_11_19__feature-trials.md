As we roll out new features limited to certain plans, we understand you may want to try them before you buy.

That's why we're introducing **14-day feature trials** you can activate whenever you want.

Feature trials can be used to enable the following:

- [Recordings](/getting-started/recordings/)
- [Storage Destinations](/guides/advanced/storage-destinations/)
- [PDF Generation](/parameters/pdf_options/)
- [Proxies](/guides/advanced/proxies/)
- [Batch Creation](/getting-started/using-the-api/#batch-image-creation)
- [Request Overrides](/parameters/request_overrides/)

New signups start with trials active. If you already have an account, open your dashboard to see what's available to try.

**[Explore your feature trials](https://htmlcsstoimage.com/dashboard/organization/trials)**

Trials use your usual credits, and there's no automatic upgrade when they end. [Learn how feature trials work](/guides/account/feature-trials/).
