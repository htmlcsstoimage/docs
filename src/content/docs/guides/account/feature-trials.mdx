**Feature trials** give your organization **14 days of access to advanced features that aren't included in your current plan**. They're available on both Free and paid plans.

Trials are shared by the organization. Your existing plan and monthly allowance continue when a feature trial ends. Trials don't add extra credits or automatically upgrade your subscription.

## View and start trials

1. [Switch to the organization](/guides/account/account-settings/#switching-organizations) you want to use.
2. Open [Feature trials in the dashboard](https://htmlcsstoimage.com/dashboard/organization/trials). You can also reach it from the Feature trials card in [organization settings](https://htmlcsstoimage.com/dashboard/organization).
3. Find an available feature and select **Start 14-day trial**. An organization **Owner or Admin** must start the trial.
4. Use the feature through the dashboard, API, or MCP, with your existing permissions.

Eligible trials can start automatically for new organizations, so check the status and dates before starting. An active trial's 14-day window is already running.

Feature trials are available on Free and paid plans for features those plans don't already include. An organization without a plan needs to choose one before starting a feature trial; Free is an option.

## Features you can try

Availability depends on your organization's plan and which trials it has already used.

| Feature | What you can try |
|:--------|:-----------------|
| [Recordings](/getting-started/recordings/) | Record HTML/CSS animations or webpages as MP4, WebM, or GIF |
| [PDF generation](/parameters/pdf_options/) | Generate PDFs from HTML/CSS and webpages |
| [Storage destinations](/guides/advanced/storage-destinations/) | Send rendered media to your own storage |
| [Proxies](/guides/advanced/proxies/) | Render webpages through your own configured proxies |
| [Batch creation](/getting-started/using-the-api/#batch-image-creation) | Create up to 25 images in one request |
| [Request overrides](/parameters/request_overrides/) | Customize network requests while rendering |

Each feature's card shows the minimum plan needed to keep using that extra feature after its trial. Upgrading is optional.

## Understand trial status

The Feature trials page shows:

- **Available for 14 days** — Your organization can start an unused trial.
- **Trial active** — The feature is available until the end date shown on the card. Dates are shown in UTC.
- **Trial expired** — Temporary access to that extra feature has ended. Your existing plan, including Free, continues.
- **Included in your plan** — You already have access through your plan and don't need a trial.

Each feature can be trialed once per organization. Changing plans doesn't restart a trial or reset its dates.

## Credits and permissions

Feature trials use your organization's normal credit allowance, and any enabled overages still apply. They don't add extra credits or replace your plan. Recording credit usage depends on duration; see [recording credits](/getting-started/recordings/#credits).

Your existing API keys and MCP connections can use active trial features within their approved permissions. A trial doesn't grant permission to manage resources or change billing. See [API key permissions](/getting-started/using-the-api/permissions/) and [MCP permissions](/integrations/mcp/permissions/).

With MCP, use the `capabilities` tool to check current feature access and trial status. Use `check_usage` to check credits used and your allowance.

## When a trial ends

**Only the extra feature access ends.** Your existing plan and its monthly allowance continue. If you're on Free, you can stay on Free and keep using its included features.

Upgrade only if you want to keep using an extra feature after its trial. Use **Upgrade your plan** at the bottom of the trials page, or [compare plans](https://htmlcsstoimage.com/pricing). There's no automatic upgrade or requirement to choose a paid plan.

If your plan already includes the feature, access continues through your plan.

## Dismiss dashboard reminders

Use the **×** on the dashboard's trial card or reminder to hide it for yourself. Dismissing a reminder doesn't end the organization's trials or hide them for other members.

You can still open the trials page from organization settings or the active/available trial count beside your subscription controls.
